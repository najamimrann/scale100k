# scale100k

Sales rescue and $100K-per-month scaling work for three Shopify stores: Pitcher of Life (POL), SolutionsHOCL (HOCL) and Life Sciences Water (LSW).

## Working documents

The full analysis and guides live in Claude Docs, where the team edits and comments on them:

- **Three-Store Synergy Plan**: a frank audit of each store, what the three plans miss when read together, the synergy model, the week 1 board, the 90-day timeline, effort allocation, the $100K math and the decisions needed from Rick.
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/cc07bebf-e2f3-47cf-8c5b-e61acc087806
- **Haider and MK Training and Job Guide**: concepts, skills-gap tests, a 12-week learning timeline, what Najam must provide, a step-by-step manual for every job MK and Haider own, and curated learning resources.
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/80b4946d-4862-4d63-82cb-c79f2b7b855e

Source plans: the POL Sales Recovery and $100K Plan (Oct 5, 2026), the SolutionsHOCL Sales Rescue and $100K Plan (Oct 5, 2026) and the LSW implementation brief (Oct 6, 2026).

## Verdict as of Oct 6, 2026

- No store reaches $100K a month in 2026. POL and HOCL together took about $2,506 in their latest 30 days. LSW has no revenue baseline yet, only about 9 orders a month.
- Priority order: POL first, HOCL second, LSW third.
- Week 1 (Oct 6 to 12) buys clean data, fixed defects and Rick's decisions. It does not buy revenue.
- Honest 90-day target: $15K to $25K a month across all three, if Meta proves an acceptable CAC.
- The three plans each assume the whole team works on one store. Run together, MK gets about 20 tasks and Haider about 27 assets in week 1. Build each fix once and copy it to all three stores.

## Corrections to the source plans

- **HOCL checkout bot.** Shopify's checkout bot protection is a Shopify Plus feature, requested through Plus Support and limited to 60-minute events. HOCL is on Basic. The training guide gives MK a method that works on Basic.
- **HOCL scorecard date.** Oct 13, 2026 is a Tuesday, not a Monday. The first Monday scorecard is Oct 12.
- **LSW claims.** NSF/ANSI 41 covers composting toilets, so the "NSF 41" wording on ten filter listings is almost certainly wrong.
